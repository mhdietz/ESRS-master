# Post-Omnibus ESRS Alignment & Materiality Mapping Matrix
**Reference Document for AI Mapping Engine ("Ibn Battuta")**
**Version: 3.1 | Basis: Commission Delegated Regulation C(2026) 5010, adopted 3 July 2026**

*Instructions for AI:* Use this matrix as a lookup reference. When evaluating a Trimble IRO, find the best semantic match within the `Sub-topic` column, then output the `ESRS Standard`, `Sub-topic`, and the standard-level `Disclosure Requirements (DRs)` for that standard. **The DR list is the full adopted DR set for the standard; actual in-scope DRs are then decided by the materiality assessment and the ESRS 1 para 24 filter — not by the sub-topic row alone.**

> **STRUCTURAL BASIS — DRs are set at standard level.** Every adopted topical standard states in its objective para 1 that if not all sub-topics are to be reported following the materiality assessment, ESRS 1 para 30 applies. DRs are therefore **not** bundled per sub-topic. Sub-topic labels below are sourced from each standard's **objective paragraph** (para 5 or 6), consistent with the non-binding overview in ESRS 1 Appendix A. E2-5's phase-in for Wave 2 "Other undertakings": quantitative SoC information may be omitted for the first three financial years of reporting (ESRS 1 para 127(d)).

**Note on Post-Omnibus Structure:** Cross-cutting disclosures on Strategy & Business Model (ESRS 2: SBM-1, SBM-2, SBM-3) and the Materiality Assessment process (ESRS 2: IRO-1, IRO-2) apply to material topics. Policies, Actions, **Metrics** and Targets are centralised under the four General Disclosure Requirements **GDR-P, GDR-A, GDR-M, GDR-T** (GDR-M is one of the four). Reminder (ESRS 1 para 24): DRs/datapoints that are **not** material shall **not** be reported, save the narrow carve-outs in ESRS 1 paras 107–108.
