## PART 1: ARCHITECTURE AND FOUNDATIONAL RULES

### 1.1 Structure of the simplified ESRS

The simplified ESRS consists of:
- **ESRS 1** — General Requirements (architecture, materiality rules, reporting obligations). Does not contain Disclosure Requirements.
- **ESRS 2** — General Disclosures (always mandatory, applies across all topics regardless of materiality outcome)
- **Topical standards** — E1 through G1 (apply only when the relevant topic is material)

Sector-specific standards have been **eliminated** under the simplified ESRS. No sector standards will be adopted.

Sub-subtopics from the 2023 ESRS have been **eliminated**. The simplified ESRS only uses topics and sub-topics.

### 1.2 Mandatory vs. materiality-conditional DRs

**Always mandatory (apply regardless of materiality assessment outcome):**
- All DRs in ESRS 2 (BP-1, BP-2, GOV-1 through GOV-4, SBM-1 through SBM-3, IRO-1, IRO-2, GDR-P, GDR-A, GDR-M, GDR-T)
- ESRS E1-2 (Identification of climate-related risks and scenario analysis) — mandatory per ESRS 2 Appendix C even if E1 is not otherwise material
- Special rule for climate: If the undertaking concludes climate change is NOT material, it must still disclose a detailed explanation of that conclusion and a forward-looking analysis of conditions that could make it material in future.

**Materiality-conditional (apply only if the relevant topic is assessed as material):**
- All other DRs in topical standards E1 through G1

**Special materiality rules in S1:**
- S1-5 (Characteristics of employees) applies whenever own workforce is material
- S1-6 (Non-employee characteristics) applies whenever non-employees are connected to material IROs
- These two DRs are still subject to the materiality of information filter

### 1.3 Phase-in provisions (simplified ESRS)

The following DRs may be omitted under phase-in provisions:
- **All DRs of E4, S2, S3, S4** — may be omitted for financial years prior to FY2027
- **All information about anticipated financial effects** (ESRS 2 para. 27 and E1-11) — may be omitted for financial years prior to FY2027, except paragraphs E1-11 38(a)(b) and 39(a)(b)
- **Quantitative information on anticipated financial effects** — may be omitted for financial years prior to FY2030, except E1-11 38(a)(b) and 39(a)(b)
- **Quantitative information on substances of concern** (E2-5) — may be omitted for financial years prior to FY2030
- **S1-6, S1-7 for non-EEA countries, S1-10, S1-11, S1-12, S1-13 para 36(d)(e) and non-employee datapoints, S1-14** — may be omitted for financial years prior to FY2027

### 1.4 Key structural changes from 2023 ESRS to simplified ESRS

| Area | 2023 ESRS | Simplified ESRS |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| Strategy & governance | Repeated in each topical standard | Consolidated entirely in ESRS 2 |
| PAT disclosures | Prescriptive sub-elements per topic | Narrative flexibility via GDR-P, GDR-A, GDR-T in ESRS 2 |
| Sector-specific standards | Planned | Eliminated |
| Sub-subtopics | Present | Eliminated — topics and sub-topics only |
| Voluntary datapoints | 269 "may disclose" datapoints | All eliminated |
| Mandatory datapoints | Baseline | Reduced by 61% |
| GHG reporting boundary | Operational control | Financial control (aligned with IFRS S2) |
| Fair presentation | Implicit | Explicit new principle in ESRS 1 |
| AR 16 topic list | Mandatory input to DMA | Non-binding guidance (still useful reference) |

### 1.5 General Disclosure Requirements for PAT (GDR-P, GDR-A, GDR-M, GDR-T)

A critical structural change: policies, actions, metrics, and targets are no longer governed by prescriptive topic-specific requirements. Instead, ESRS 2 provides four General Disclosure Requirements that apply across all material topics:

- **GDR-P** — General Disclosure Requirement for Policies: For each material topic, describe policies adopted to manage material impacts, risks and opportunities. Narrative approach; no mandatory sub-elements specified per topic.
- **GDR-A** — General Disclosure Requirement for Actions and Resources: Describe key actions taken and resources allocated. May reference actions across topics if they serve multiple purposes.
- **GDR-M** — General Disclosure Requirement for Metrics: Disclose metrics that are material for understanding the undertaking's performance on material topics.
- **GDR-T** — General Disclosure Requirement for Targets: Disclose time-bound, outcome-oriented targets for material topics.

In topical standards, DRs for policies, actions, and targets reference GDR-P, GDR-A, and GDR-T rather than containing standalone requirements. The topical DRs for PAT add topic-specific context (e.g., E1-4 specifies that climate policies cover mitigation and adaptation) but the core disclosure logic sits in ESRS 2.

## PART 2: ESRS 2 — GENERAL DISCLOSURES (Always Mandatory)

**Full name:** ESRS 2 General Disclosures
**Applicability:** Always mandatory. All DRs apply regardless of the outcome of the materiality assessment.
**Source:** EFRAG Knowledge Hub, Simplified ESRS, Technical Advice 30 November 2025

### Basis for Preparation

| DR Code | DR Name | Disclosure Objective | Key Content |
|---------|---------|---------------------|-------------|
| BP-1 | Basis for preparation of the sustainability statement | Enable understanding of the basis on which the sustainability statement is prepared | Statement of compliance with ESRS; any reliefs or options applied; scope of consolidation; list of DRs complied with; list of supplementary information |
| BP-2 | Specific information if phasing-in options are used | Enable understanding of which phase-in provisions have been applied | Which DRs have been omitted under phase-in; whether omitted topics have been assessed as material |

### Governance

| DR Code | DR Name | Disclosure Objective | Key Content |
|---------|---------|---------------------|-------------|
| GOV-1 | Role of administrative, management and supervisory bodies (AMSB) in relation to sustainability | Enable understanding of governance roles and responsibilities | Composition of AMSB incl. % independent members, % by gender; roles and responsibilities of AMSB re sustainability; oversight processes |
| GOV-2 | Integration of sustainability-related performance in incentive schemes | Enable understanding of how sustainability is integrated in remuneration | Whether and how sustainability performance is linked to incentive schemes for AMSB members and senior executives |
| GOV-3 | Statement on due diligence | Enable understanding of due diligence processes | Description of due diligence process related to material sustainability matters |
| GOV-4 | Risk management and internal controls over sustainability reporting | Enable understanding of risk management and internal controls | How risk management and internal control processes relate to sustainability reporting |

### Strategy

| DR Code | DR Name | Disclosure Objective | Key Content |
|---------|---------|---------------------|-------------|
| SBM-1 | Strategy, business model and value chain | Enable understanding of business model and value chain | Description of business model; key products/services; markets; value chain; how strategy relates to sustainability |
| SBM-2 | Interests and views of stakeholders | Enable understanding of how stakeholder views are considered | Key stakeholders identified; engagement approach; how interests and views are reflected in strategy |
| SBM-3 | Interaction of material impacts, risks and opportunities with strategy and business model, and financial effects | Enable understanding of how material IROs interact with strategy and their current financial effects | Description of each material IRO; how strategy and business model respond; current financial effects including carrying value of assets/liabilities affected |
| IRO-1 | Description of process to identify and assess material impacts, risks and opportunities and material information to be reported | Enable understanding of DMA process | Description of DMA process; how topics and sub-topics were considered; engagement with stakeholders; how thresholds were set; how entity-specific topics were identified |
| IRO-2 | Material impacts, risks and opportunities and DRs included in the sustainability statement | Enable understanding of what is and is not being reported | List of all material IROs; DRs included in sustainability statement; DRs omitted and why; for topics assessed as not material, brief explanation |

### General Disclosure Requirements for Policies, Actions, Metrics and Targets

| DR Code | DR Name | When it Applies |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| GDR-P | General Disclosure Requirement for Policies | For each material topic where the undertaking has adopted policies |
| GDR-A | General Disclosure Requirement for Actions and Resources | For each material topic where the undertaking has taken or planned actions |
| GDR-M | General Disclosure Requirement for Metrics | For each material topic — disclose metrics that are material |
| GDR-T | General Disclosure Requirement for Targets | For each material topic — disclose time-bound, outcome-oriented targets |

**Note on GDR-P/A/T when no policy/action/target exists:** If the undertaking has not adopted a policy, implemented an action, or set a target for a material topic, it shall disclose this fact and may indicate a timeframe for when it plans to do so.

### E1 — Climate Change

**Full name:** ESRS E1 Climate Change
**Applicability:** When climate change relates to material impacts, risks or opportunities
**Sub-topics:** Climate change mitigation | Climate change adaptation | Energy
**Source:** EFRAG Knowledge Hub, Simplified ESRS E1, Technical Advice 30 November 2025

**Special mandatory rule:** E1-2 (climate risk identification and scenario analysis) is mandatory per ESRS 2 Appendix C even if climate change is assessed as not otherwise material. If climate change is assessed as NOT material overall, the undertaking must still explain this conclusion in detail with a forward-looking analysis.

#### E1 Strategy DRs

| DR Code | DR Name | Sub-topics | Disclosure Objective | Key Content |
|---------|---------|-----------|---------------------|-------------|
| E1-1 | Transition plan for climate change mitigation | Mitigation | Enable understanding of past, current and future mitigation efforts for compatibility with 1.5°C transition | Key features of transition plan: GHG reduction targets, decarbonisation levers, key actions and investments, board approval, alignment with business strategy; CapEx related to coal/oil/gas if applicable; key assumptions and dependencies; locked-in GHG emission assessment; progress in implementing the plan. If no transition plan, disclose this fact and indicate if/when one is expected. |
| E1-2 | Identification of climate-related risks and scenario analysis | Mitigation, Adaptation, Energy | Enable understanding of how the undertaking identifies and assesses climate-related risks (MANDATORY regardless of materiality) | For each material climate-related risk: whether classified as physical or transition risk; methodology to assess exposure and sensitivity of assets and business activities to climate risks over short/medium/long term; results of climate resilience analysis; implications for strategy and business model |
| E1-3 | Resilience in relation to climate change | Mitigation, Adaptation | Enable understanding of resilience of strategy and business model to climate-related risks | Results of climate resilience analysis; how scenario analysis informs potential responses; how transition plan and mitigation/adaptation actions contribute to resilience; capacity to adjust strategy and business model |

| DR Code | DR Name | Sub-topics | Disclosure Objective | Key Content |

| E1-5 | Actions and resources in relation to climate change mitigation and adaptation | Mitigation, Adaptation, Energy | Per GDR-A | Key actions and resources allocated for climate change mitigation and adaptation, including energy efficiency and renewable energy deployment. #### E1 Metrics and Targets DRs

| DR Code | DR Name | Sub-topics | Disclosure Objective | Key Content |
|---------|---------|-----------|---------------------|-------------|
| E1-6 | Targets related to climate change | Mitigation, Adaptation, Energy | Enable understanding of targets set for managing climate-related IROs | GHG emission reduction targets (absolute and/or intensity); energy efficiency and renewable energy targets; adaptation targets; whether targets are science-based |

| E1-10 | Internal carbon pricing | Mitigation | Enable understanding of internal carbon pricing mechanisms | Description of internal carbon pricing mechanism and price applied. Only applies if the undertaking has implemented such a mechanism. |
| E1-11 | Anticipated financial effects from material physical and transition risks and potential climate-related opportunities | Mitigation, Adaptation, Energy | Enable understanding of anticipated financial effects | Quantitative and qualitative disclosure of anticipated financial effects from physical risks (assets exposed), transition risks, and potential opportunities. Quantitative information required unless financial effects not separately identifiable or measurement uncertainty too high. Phase-in available: full DR may be omitted prior to FY2027; quantitative information may be omitted prior to FY2030 (except certain asset exposure datapoints). |

## PART 4: TOPIC → DR MAPPING LOGIC (Q&A ID 177 Cascade — Simplified ESRS)

### 4.1 Cascading approach

When an IRO is identified as material, the mapping follows this cascade:

**Step 1 — ESRS Standard:** Which topical standard(s) does this IRO map to? An IRO may map to multiple standards (primary and secondary).

**Step 2 — Sub-topic:** Within each triggered standard, which sub-topic(s) does this IRO relate to? Only the material sub-topic needs to be reported — if an IRO relates only to climate change mitigation, the undertaking need not report on adaptation or energy sub-topics unless separately material.

**Step 3 — DRs triggered:** For the triggered standard and sub-topic(s):
- Strategy DRs (if the IRO informs strategy or transition planning)
- PAT DRs (GDR-P, GDR-A, GDR-T via topical references — always apply to material topics)
- Metrics DRs (apply if the specific metric is material for understanding performance)
- Anticipated financial effects DR (if financial effects are material and quantifiable)

**Step 4 — ESRS 2 DRs always apply:** IRO-1 (DMA process), IRO-2 (list of material matters), SBM-3 (IRO interaction with strategy and financial effects) are always triggered alongside any material topical DR.

**Step 5 — Phase-in check:** Confirm whether any triggered DRs are subject to phase-in provisions given the undertaking's reporting timeline.

## PART 5: IRO TYPE → ESRS STANDARD ROUTING RULES

These rules are used to auto-match an IRO to candidate ESRS standards before human review. Rules are additive — an IRO may match multiple standards.

| AI regulations, ESG regulations, regulatory compliance | G1 (governance/conduct primary) |
| data breach, cybersecurity | S4 (consumer data impact primary), G1 (governance secondary) |

### 5.2 IRO type routing modifiers

| IRO Type | Routing modifier |
|----------|-----------------|
| Impact | Prioritise social and environmental standards (E1–E5, S1–S4) where impact is on people or environment; ESRS 2 SBM-3 always triggered |
| Risk (financial) | Prioritise financial effects DRs (E1-11, E2-6, E3-5, E4-6, E5-6, ESRS 2 SBM-3); governance DRs (GOV-4 for risk management) |
| Opportunity | Prioritise strategy DRs (SBM-1, SBM-3); transition plan DRs if climate-related (E1-1); financial effects DRs |

### 5.3 Entity-specific IRO handling

When an IRO does not map cleanly to any standard in AR 16 (i.e., the topic is not covered or not covered with sufficient granularity by ESRS), the following apply:
- ESRS 1 paragraph 11: The undertaking shall provide entity-specific disclosures
- ESRS 2 BP-1: The list of entity-specific disclosures must be documented in the basis for preparation
- ESRS 2 IRO-2: Entity-specific material matters must be listed alongside standard-based DRs
- GDR-P, GDR-A, GDR-M, GDR-T still apply — policies, actions, metrics and targets should be described for entity-specific material topics using the same narrative GDR framework
- Examples from this DMA: "Emerging Tech & AI", "Regulatory Compliance" (broad) — these map partially to G1 but may have entity-specific dimensions that require additional disclosure

## PART 7: KNOWN UNCERTAINTIES AND FLAGGED ITEMS FOR REVIEW

The following items are flagged because the final Delegated Act may differ from the December 2025 technical advice, or because EFRAG's guidance on these points was ambiguous at the time this knowledge base was compiled.

| Item | Uncertainty | Action |
|------|-------------|--------|
| GHG boundary (E1-8) | Simplified ESRS changes boundary to financial control. Some commentators note this creates interoperability issues with GHG Protocol and IFRS S2. Final Delegated Act may clarify. | Use financial control boundary per technical advice; flag for update when Delegated Act published |
| Anticipated financial effects (E1-11, E2-6 etc.) | Technical advice requires both quantitative and qualitative where feasible, but exemptions available. Interaction with IFRS S2 anticipated financial effects requirements is being monitored. | Apply narrative approach with quantitative where possible; document exemption basis if quantitative omitted |
| Phase-in for entities other than wave 1 | EFRAG's technical advice notes the decision on phase-in provisions for non-wave-1 entities is left to the European Commission. | Flag in output; confirm with client which wave they are and monitor Delegated Act |
